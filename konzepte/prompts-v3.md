## STYLE (gemeinsam)
Website storyboard sheet: one single image, a 3x3 grid of nine 16:9 panels on a near-black background with thin gutters, a small white panel number (1-9) in the top-left corner of each panel. Every panel is a photorealistic screen state of the same ultra-luxury website for the Russian, Georgian and Armenian restaurant DR. SCHIWAGO in Duisburg, the name spelled exactly D-R-dot S-C-H-I-W-A-G-O. The reference image shows the real rooms and dishes of this restaurant (crystal chandelier in a dark room, banquet hall with backlit amber onyx wall, the same hall at night in violet light, shashlik over charcoal, layered herring salad, covered terrace with string lights); use exactly these rooms and dishes as the photography. Food is shown only as single, beautifully plated dishes with a lot of dark space around them, never crowded buffet platters, never many dishes at once. The design feels quieter, more refined and more expensive than a typical restaurant website: generous negative space, small refined typography, hairline details, slow cinematic photography, nothing loud, no chunky buttons, no gradient text. Short German headlines only, spelled exactly as given. No prices, no euro signs, no other logos. Ultra sharp, consistent across all nine panels.

## PANELS (gemeinsam)
Panel 1: full-screen hero with NO food at all: the dark elegant room with the glowing crystal chandelier and a soft amber onyx glow, mostly calm dark negative space, small centered wordmark DR. SCHIWAGO, one line "Jeden Samstag ab 18 Uhr", a small hairline button "Tisch reservieren". Panel 2: scroll scene, one dish very large (shashlik over glowing charcoal), a small numeral "01" and the dish name "Schaschlik" in elegant serif. Panel 3: a menu-card section titled "Das Buffet" with dish names set like a printed menu and two small photos. Panel 4: a sparse editorial gallery with three single plated dishes on dark plates, lots of space between them, titled "Aus unserer Küche". Panel 5: the banquet hall split by a fine vertical hairline with a small round handle, left by day, right at night in violet light, small labels "Tag" and "Nacht". Panel 6: events section, a festive wedding table, headline "Hochzeiten". Panel 7: catering page, headline "Festliches Buffet" with five courses as a refined selectable list. Panel 8: reservation, a refined card with the fields "Datum" and "Personen" and the button "Tisch reservieren". Panel 9: a hand holding a smartphone showing the same hero.

## DSSB-4 „Champagner“
Style: warm ivory paper background alternating with deep black full-bleed photo sections, champagne gold hairlines, thin high-contrast Didone serif headlines in deep black, tiny letter-spaced sans-serif captions, a magazine-like editorial grid like a Michelin-starred restaurant or a luxury hotel website.

## DSSB-5 „Schwarzer Samt“
Style: almost entirely deep black, very restrained, small centered thin serif headlines in soft ivory, single champagne gold hairlines and small gold numerals, full-bleed slow cinematic dark food photography with deep shadows, typography like an elegant printed menu card, like the website of an exclusive fine dining restaurant.

## DSSB-6 „Grand Imperial“
Style: deep bordeaux velvet and black with antique gold and ivory, very fine gold line frames and small art-deco ornaments inspired by Russian imperial elegance, thin serif headlines, ivory text, a grand hotel atmosphere, still minimal and calm.
