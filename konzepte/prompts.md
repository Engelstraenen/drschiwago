# DR. SCHIWAGO – Storyboard-Prompts (sjinn Nano Banana 2.1, 16:9, 2K, Unlimited, Referenz: referenz-echte-raeume.jpg)

## STYLE (in jedem Prompt nach „DSSB-x Name.“)
Website storyboard sheet: one single image, a 3x3 grid of nine 16:9 panels on a near-black background with thin gutters, a small white panel number (1-9) in the top-left corner of each panel. Every panel is a photorealistic, high-end screen state of the same award-winning website for the restaurant and Saturday night venue DR. SCHIWAGO in Duisburg, the name spelled exactly D-R-dot S-C-H-I-W-A-G-O. The reference image shows the real rooms of this venue: banquet hall with a glowing backlit amber onyx wall and long tables with white tablecloths, the karaoke lounge with a crystal chandelier and velvet sofas, a golden gorilla statue in a flower garden, a glass-roof veranda with a flower chandelier, a backlit onyx bar, buffet platters. Use exactly these real rooms, materials and colors as the photography inside the web design. Cinematic lighting, premium Awwwards-style layout, elegant high-contrast serif headlines plus a clean sans-serif, short German headlines only, spelled exactly as given. No prices, no euro signs, no other brand logos. Ultra sharp, consistent design across all nine panels.

SCHIWAGO, a deep red button "Tisch reservieren", clock 18:00 bottom left. Panel 3: close tracking shot along the buffet with shashlik skewers, plov, red caviar canapes, pirozhki, giant kinetic number "50+" sliding in with the word "Gerichte". Panel 4: clock 20:00, the glass-roof veranda with the flower chandelier at blue hour, colorful cocktails on the bar, headline "Drei Bars." Panel 5: the golden gorilla statue in the flower garden at blue hour with fairy lights, headline "Kong Garten". Panel 6: clock 22:00, the banquet hall now dark with violet and red light washing over the walls, a DJ booth and dancing guests in evening wear, headline "Licht runter." Panel 7: the karaoke lounge with the crystal chandelier, a silver microphone sharp in the foreground, headline "Karaoke an." Panel 8: clock 02:00, last glasses and candles on the onyx bar, headline "Bis nächsten Samstag." and an elegant reservation card with fields Datum and Personen and a red button "Tisch reservieren". Panel 9: a smartphone held in a hand showing the same website at 22:00 in violet light with a sticky red button "Tisch reservieren".
