## Prompts
- DSM-01: Photorealistic 4K photograph for the hero section of a luxury restaurant website. A large multi-tiered crystal chandelier with warm golden light hangs at the top center of the frame in an otherwise completely dark black room, it fills only the upper quarter of the image, its top touches the upper edge. Below it a large empty deep black area in the middle of the frame. At the very bottom a glossy black lacquer floor shows a soft mirrored reflection of the chandelier that fades into black. Sparkling crystal prisms, warm gold highlights, a faint golden haze around the light, rich deep blacks, perfectly centered symmetrical composition, cinematic and elegant. The chandelier has the same crystal style as the chandelier in the reference photo.
- DSM-02: Photorealistic 4K interior photograph of the exact banquet hall from the reference photo: the long backlit amber onyx wall glowing warm gold in horizontal bands, dark upholstered wall panels, rows of tables with white tablecloths and white napkins folded upright, dark chairs, dark ceiling with small warm spotlights. Evening atmosphere just before the guests arrive, dim and moody with deep shadows, warm light only from the onyx wall and small spots. Wide-angle view from slightly above table height looking diagonally along the room, the glowing onyx wall runs across the upper half, the lower third darker and calm. Keep the real room, materials and colors exactly as in the reference, only sharper and more cinematic.
- DSM-03: Edit the reference image. Keep everything exactly the same (room, camera, tables, chairs, napkins, walls, onyx wall, ceiling). Change only the lighting to a party night: onyx wall and ceiling glow deep violet and magenta, purple light over tables and chairs, a few small violet beams and light haze, darker overall.
- DSM-05a: exact veranda bar from the reference: glass roof with metal frame, large hanging flower chandelier, plants, bar counter, tables. Blue hour, warm lights inside, portrait.
- DSM-05b: exact main hall bar from the reference: long counter with glowing backlit golden onyx front, lit bottle shelves, polished floor reflecting gold. Evening, centered portrait.
- DSM-05c: exact karaoke bar from the reference: counter with bar stools in warm light, bottles and glasses, lounge next to the karaoke room, hint of violet, portrait.
- DSM-06: chrome vocal microphone on a slim stand entering diagonally from lower right, grille pointing up-left, razor sharp; background the karaoke lounge from the reference as large violet/magenta/amber/teal/gold bokeh with chandelier sparkle; upper half calm and dark.
- DSM-07: deep black surface with very subtle embossed baroque leaf damask in dark antique gold, mainly right third, fading to black left/center, flat frontal.

- DSM-08b: polished black leather oxford shoes on dark glossy surface, warm golden rim light, black background.
- DSM-08c: two crystal flutes with sparkling wine and bubbles on a dark bar counter, warm golden bokeh.
